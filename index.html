Welcome

The internet has become an important part of our daily life. We use it for education, communication, entertainment and work.

A responsible netizen is a person who uses the internet safely, respectfully and wisely.

Think Before You Click!

🛡️ Online Safety

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Create strong passwords and avoid using the same password for every account.

🔑 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

🎣 Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click links received from unknown or suspicious sources.

🔒 Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing personal information publicly on the internet.

Responsible Social Media Use

Good uses

  • Learning new skills
  • Educational information
  • Communication with friends and family
  • Career and professional networking

Things to Avoid

  • Excessive scrolling
  • Sharing private information
  • Believing every post is true
  • Spreading rumours

Fake News and Misinformation

Fake news is false or misleading information shared as if it were true.

Before sharing any information, check the source, date and other reliable sources.
